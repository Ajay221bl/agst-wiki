These are my study notes for a graduate course on the principles of experimentation (AGST, Fall 2026): probability, describing data, study design, inference, t-tests and one-way ANOVA, all run in R. They are written from first principles. Each note asks not just "what is X" but "why do we do it this way, and what would go wrong if we didn't".

## How the notes fit together
- **Atomic notes** explain one idea each, starting from zero and building step by step from simpler notes, with a figure and a small worked example.
- **Molecular notes** ("Why ...") answer one "why" question by combining several atomic notes.
- **Maps of Content** group the notes by topic and give a reading order.

- **Rusty on maths symbols? Start with [[MOC - Basics from Zero]].** It explains $\sum$, squares, Greek letters and `2.2e-16` from scratch.

- **Choosing a test?** Use the [[Test Choice Guide]] and the [[Formula Sheet]].
- **Want to browse everything?** See [[All Notes]] and the [[References]].
