The notes cite these by author, year and chapter, usually with a page number.

## Vu and Harrington 2020
Vu, J., and D. Harrington. 2020. *Introductory Statistics for the Life and Biomedical Sciences*. First Edition. OpenIntro. https://www.openintro.org/book/biostat

The main textbook. It is free and openly licensed from OpenIntro, and so are the datasets used in the worked examples (`famuss`, `swim`, `stem_cells`), which are in the `Data` folder.

## Kirk 2013
Kirk, R. E. 2013. *Experimental Design: Procedures for the Behavioral Sciences*. 4th edition. SAGE Publications, Thousand Oaks, CA. Chapter 1.

## Wasserstein and Lazar 2016
Wasserstein, R. L., and N. A. Lazar. 2016. The ASA's statement on p-values: context, process, and purpose. *The American Statistician* 70(2):129-133. https://doi.org/10.1080/00031305.2016.1154108
