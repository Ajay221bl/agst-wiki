> [!tip] New to this?
> Start here if symbols, squares or spreadsheets feel shaky. Every later note assumes these ideas, and each one takes only a few minutes.

The small pieces of maths and data vocabulary that statistics uses without stopping to explain them.

Most people who find statistics hard aren't struggling with statistics. They're tripping over a symbol nobody explained, like $\sum$, or a square root that appeared without a reason. Fix those and the real ideas become visible. None of this is advanced. It's just the alphabet, and once you can read it, the formulas turn into sentences.

## 2. Numbers and symbols

- [[Squares and Square Roots]]: why the variance squares distances, and why the standard deviation takes the root to undo it

- [[Inequality Signs]]: <, ≤, >, ≥ and ≠, and whether the boundary value counts
- [[Greek Letters and Symbols]]: Greek letters for the population you can't see, ordinary letters for the sample you can
- [[Scientific Notation]]: how to read `2.2e-16` in R output, a tiny number, not a large one
